# Scare payout

Any monster can scare any bedroom. Monster rarity does not have to match the bedroom tier.

**Fear per scare = monster multiplier × accessory multiplier × bedroom tier multiplier**

- Monster multiplier comes from its [[Rarity tiers|rarity tier]].
- Accessory multiplier comes from the [[Accessories|accessory]] it wears.
- Bedroom tier multiplier comes from the random tier of the [[Bedrooms and tiers|bedroom]].

Open: does a [[Manual scares|manual scare]] pay extra? See [[Open design questions]].

Source: [[Game design doc]].
