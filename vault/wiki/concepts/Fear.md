# Fear

Fear is the main currency. Monsters earn it by scaring guests (see [[Scare payout]]), including while the player is offline.

Spent on: [[Eggs]], [[Accessories|accessory boxes]], more [[Bedrooms and tiers|bedrooms]], and [[Rebirth]].

The other currency is Robux (see [[Monetization]]).
