# Platform layout

Based on a slot-tycoon reference screenshot:

- A center aisle runs from the platform entrance to the back.
- 3 [[Bedrooms and tiers|bedrooms]] on each side, so 6 per floor.
- Each bedroom's showcase pad (where the monster stands) faces the aisle.
- The player walks in from the aisle for a [[Manual scares|manual scare]].
- Bedrooms past 6 (from [[Rebirth]]) go on a 2nd floor.
