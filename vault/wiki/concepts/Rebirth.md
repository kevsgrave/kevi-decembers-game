# Rebirth

- Costs [[Fear]]. Later rebirths also need a set number of monsters in the [[Monster index]].
- Unlocks another [[Bedrooms and tiers|bedroom]] slot. After all 6 on floor 1, new ones go on floor 2 (see [[Platform layout]]).

Open: what resets on rebirth? See [[Open design questions]].
