# Bedrooms and tiers

Each player has a platform of bedrooms. A [[Guests|guest]] sleeps in each one.

- After every scare the bedroom respawns with a new tier. The tier is pure luck; there are no luck upgrades.
- [[Fear]] buys more bedrooms; [[Rebirth]] adds bedroom slots.
- Players build the bedroom sets themselves.

Layout: see [[Platform layout]].
