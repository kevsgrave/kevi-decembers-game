# Eggs

Monsters come from eggs. There is one egg system, because monsters are also the workers.

- Bought with [[Fear]] or Robux.
- Some special eggs are buried around the map and dug up (see [[Exploring and egg digging]]).
- Each hatch rolls a monster from the [[Rarity tiers]] and fills the [[Monster index]].

Source: [[Game design doc]].
