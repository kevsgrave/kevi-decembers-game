# Kid-friendly scares

The audience is young kids, so scares stay mild and cartoony to keep the Roblox maturity rating low.

- Scares are seen through a bedroom window.
- No gore, no intense scares, no loud audio, no screen flashes.
- Famous creepypastas that are gory get a kid-friendly redesign.
