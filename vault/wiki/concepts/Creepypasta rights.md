# Creepypasta rights

Not every creepypasta is free to use. Check each monster's source before adding it.

- Slender Man is copyrighted. Others belong to their authors.
- SCP characters are CC BY-SA and need credit.
- When in doubt, design an original monster.

Each monster page has a `rights_checked` field for this.
