# Core loop

The main thing players do, over and over:

1. Hatch a monster from an [[Eggs|egg]].
2. Put it on the showcase pad in front of a [[Bedrooms and tiers|bedroom]]. One monster per bedroom.
3. The monster scares the sleeping [[Guests|guest]] by itself in about 5 seconds. Players watch through the bedroom window.
4. The scare pays [[Fear]] (see [[Scare payout]]) and the bedroom respawns with a new random tier.
5. Spend Fear on eggs, [[Accessories|accessory boxes]] and more bedrooms.

Related: [[Manual scares]], [[Rebirth]], [[Kid-friendly scares]].
