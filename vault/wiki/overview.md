# Jumpscare RNG

A Roblox tycoon/RNG game for young kids by Kevi & Decembers.

You run a platform of bedrooms. Creepypasta monsters stand in front of each bedroom and scare the animal guests sleeping inside. Every scare earns [[Fear]] and rerolls the bedroom into a new random tier. Spend Fear to hatch more monsters and unlock more bedrooms.
