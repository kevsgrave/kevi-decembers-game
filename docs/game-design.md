# Jumpscare RNG: game design

Working design for the game, from the idea and decisions on 2026-10-25. Open questions are listed at the end.

## Pitch

You run a platform of bedrooms. Creepypasta monsters sit in front of each bedroom and scare the animal guests sleeping inside. Every scare earns Fear and rerolls the bedroom into a new random tier. Spend Fear to hatch more monsters and unlock more bedrooms.

## Core loop

1. Hatch a monster from an egg (Fear or Robux).
2. Place it on the showcase platform in front of a bedroom. One monster per bedroom.
3. The monster scares the guest automatically. Players watch through the bedroom window.
4. The scare pays Fear and the bedroom respawns as a new randomly rolled tier.
5. Spend Fear on eggs, accessory boxes and more bedrooms.

## Scare payout

Any monster can scare any bedroom; monster rarity does not need to match bedroom tier.

Fear per scare = monster multiplier × accessory multiplier × bedroom tier multiplier

## Monsters

- Monsters are the workers. There is one egg system.
- Monsters are creepypasta characters, redesigned to be kid friendly.
- 6 rarity tiers plus Secret.
- About 100 monsters at launch, provided by the designer.
- A monster index tracks which ones a player has collected.

## Guests and bedrooms

- Guests are original animal characters.
- Bedrooms live on the player's own platform and respawn with a new tier after each scare.
- Platform layout: a center aisle runs from the platform entrance to the back, with 3 bedrooms on each side (6 per floor). Each bedroom's showcase pad sits on the aisle side, and the player walks in from the aisle to do a manual scare. The layout follows the slot-tycoon style of a center walkway with slots on both sides.
- Scares are seen through a window. No gore, no intense scares, no loud audio, no screen flashes.

## Manual scares

- An optional, just-for-fun mini mode: the player enters a bedroom and does the scare themselves.
- The tutorial teaches the game through this mode.

## Accessories

- Accessory boxes are sold in a rotating shop.
- An accessory goes on a monster (one per monster) to raise its multiplier, or the player can wear it.

## Progression

- Fear buys more bedrooms.
- Rebirth unlocks another bedroom slot. Once all 6 bedrooms on the first floor are open, the next ones go on a 2nd floor.

## Multiplayer

- Shared servers with several player platforms.

## Monetization

- Eggs can be bought with Fear or Robux.

## Audience and content rules

- Target audience: young kids.
- Keep scares cartoony so the Roblox maturity rating stays low.

## Risks

- Not every creepypasta is free to use. Some have owners (Slender Man is copyrighted; others belong to their authors). SCP characters are CC BY-SA and need credit. Check each monster's source before adding it, or design original ones.
- Several famous creepypastas are gory and need a kid-friendly redesign.
